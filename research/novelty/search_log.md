### C1: Homeostatic Surprise-Gated Associative Memory (HSAM)
**Mechanism (as prototyped):** fixed random projection + fixed-capacity prototype/slot memory (keys/values, utility, fill mask), k-winner similarity retrieval, local delta updates to winner keys/values, surprise/mismatch/low-similarity-gated allocation, utility decay/replacement, and bounded endogenous homeostatic tuning of learning rates/vigilance-like thresholds from error/improvement EMAs. Backprop-free, online, CPU-cheap.

## Synthesis and selection implications
- **Closest prior-art families for HSAM:** ART (vigilance/resonance, self-adjusting vigilance variants), GNG/GNG-U and SOINN/E-SOINN (incremental prototype allocation + utility/pruning), prototype/associative memories and Hebbian readouts (e.g. RECAP-style local prototype updates), and TTT/fast-weights as a different (gradient-based) online-adaptation family.
- **Honest novelty assessment:** no candidate in this set currently has evidence of being fundamentally new. **HSAM is best treated as [CONJECTURE -> INCREMENTAL MECHANISTIC COMBINATION]**: a specific backprop-free slot-memory design combining fixed random projection, k-winner local delta updates, mismatch/surprise/low-similarity allocation, utility replacement, and endogenous error/improvement-driven homeostasis. Novelty claim must be limited to this exact combination and supported by ablations against nearest conceptual baselines (e.g. frozen-threshold/no-allocation/no-tuning variants) plus careful positioning against ART/GNG/SOINN.
- **Recommended focus given existing evidence:** HSAM has the strongest preliminary empirical signal in saved results (`research/03_experiments/hsam_digits/*`, `hsam_self_improve_stream/*`) and is cheapest/most testable on 2 vCPU; prioritize rigorous validation, ablations, and honest comparison/positioning over broadening to weakly novel candidates. C5/C8 remain secondary hypotheses pending implementation.
